Cyclop for Windows 11 (x64) 0.2.2: Cyclop follows you: the mouse, your typing, and (opt-in) where you look.

### Downloads

- **CyclopWindows.exe**: standalone executable; download and run. No Python installation required. Its first launch can take longer while bundled files are extracted.
- **CyclopWindows-win-x64.zip**: portable folder build. Extract it and run `CyclopWindows/CyclopWindows.exe`, keeping `_internal` alongside it. Use this option for faster startup.
- **SHA256SUMS.txt**: checksums for both downloads.

Quit the previous copy from its tray menu before launching this version. Existing local notes, snippets and settings are preserved. The builds are unsigned.

### New

- **Follows the mouse pointer anywhere on the screen**, and reads along while you type.
- **Look where I look** (opt-in, Settings → Cyclop mascot): with your webcam, Cyclop looks at you and learns where on the screen you are looking. It calibrates itself from your mouse movements and keeps improving; accuracy is roughly a screen region. The camera runs only while the option is on and Cyclop is visible; nothing is saved or sent.
- **More life**: wake-up intro, startle when the pointer darts at it, dizzy when you circle the pointer around it, annoyed at rapid clicks, leaning in to inspect, hunting spots on the screen with sonar, and yawning before it falls asleep with floating z's.

### Fixes included

- Tucking away is clean: the eye slides fully above the screen edge, leaving the stats pill (with a red eye dot) or a slim red handle, instead of hanging half off the screen.

### Validation

Automated functionality and behaviour tests, gaze-calibration tests, Windows desktop interaction checks (including a real mouse click on Cyclop), native WinRT and OCR checks, and startup/clean-exit checks for both packaged executables run before publication. The face detector model is downloaded at build time and verified against a pinned SHA-256.
